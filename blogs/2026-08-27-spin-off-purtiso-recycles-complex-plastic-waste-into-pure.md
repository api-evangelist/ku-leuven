In recent years, a unique chemical process has been developed at KU Leuven’s Faculty of Bioscience Engineering. This process breaks down polyurethane, turning it back into its pure building blocks. The new KU Leuven spin-off Purtiso will now scale up the process, and introduce it to the market.

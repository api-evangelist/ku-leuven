KU Leuven donates 25,000 euros to humanitarian organisations providing relief for victims of the devastating floods in Nepal and Tibet. The donation is allocated equally to MSF (doctors without borders) and the Red Cross, two organisations that provide emergency assistance to the population affected by the floods.

KU Leuven has formally inaugurated the Leuven Lille Louvain Health Alliance together with University Hospitals Leuven, Université de Lille, CHU Lille, UCLouvain and Cliniques universitaires Saint-Luc. With this alliance, universities and university hospitals are pooling their expertise to further strengthen research, education, and innovation in health sciences.

The Sun continuously blows a stream of charged particles into space: the solar wind. This stream fills our entire Solar System and also affects the space around Earth. Using extremely large-scale computer simulations, KU Leuven researchers show how electrons in the solar wind can develop a striking high-energy “tail”.

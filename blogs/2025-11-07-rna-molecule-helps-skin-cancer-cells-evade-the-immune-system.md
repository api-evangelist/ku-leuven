date: "2025-11-07"

Researchers at KU Leuven have identified an RNA molecule that enables melanoma to hide from the immune system. When this molecule is blocked, immune cells are once again able to detect and attack the tumour. These findings offer promising directions for improving current immunotherapy treatments.

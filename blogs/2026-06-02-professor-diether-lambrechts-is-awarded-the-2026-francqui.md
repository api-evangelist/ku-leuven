Professor Diether Lambrechts and Professor Patrice Cani are the laureates of the 2026 Francqui-Collen Prize, one of Belgium’s highest scientific awards. The award was presented to them by King Philippe. Professor Lambrechts receives the award for his groundbreaking genetic research into cancer treatments.

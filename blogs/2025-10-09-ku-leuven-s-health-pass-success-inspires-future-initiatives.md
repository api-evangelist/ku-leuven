During the KU Leuven Inside weekend in February 2025, visitors took part in a series of 14 simple tests to assess their health. Participants have now received their personal results and can compare them with those of their peers via a dedicated website. The project demonstrates how citizen science can serve as a powerful catalyst for public health research.

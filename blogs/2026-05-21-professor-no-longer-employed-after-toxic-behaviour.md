At the end of 2023, KU Leuven received a report of a conflict in certain research teams at the Rega Institute. KU Leuven intervened immediately, organising initial internal dialogue with the staff members and colleagues involved. In addition, a RASAS risk analysis (risk analysis for a specific work situation) was carried out by the external service for prevention and protection at work IDEWE.

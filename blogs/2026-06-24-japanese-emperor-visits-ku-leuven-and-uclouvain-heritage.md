During his state visit to Belgium, Japanese Emperor Naruhito made a stop in Leuven to visit the University. Together with King Filip, the emperor highlighted the long-standing relationship between Leuven and Japan and explored a selection from the academic heritage of KU Leuven and UCLouvain.

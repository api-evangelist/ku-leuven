Researchers at KU Leuven have developed a technique that allows genetically modified bacteria to grow in a controlled and temporary way, instead of the traditional pattern of exponential growth. The proof-of-concept study puts a limit on bacterial growth, opening up possibilities for the safe use of bacteria in, for example, medicine or agriculture.

What new insights can a fossil unearthed more than a century ago still provide? Quite a few, according to a new study. A research team led by scientists from KU Leuven and the Royal Belgian Institute of Natural Sciences used CT scans to look inside the skulls of fossil crocodilians that lived approximately 80 to 60 million years ago.

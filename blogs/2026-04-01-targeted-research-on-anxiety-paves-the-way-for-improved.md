KU Leuven researchers are mapping how experimental research on anxiety-related symptoms can be conducted in a more reliable and clinically relevant way. The insights can eventually contribute to more effective treatment for people with anxiety disorders, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and post-traumatic stress disorder.

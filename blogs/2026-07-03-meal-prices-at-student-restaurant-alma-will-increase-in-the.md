Starting from the 2026-2027 academic year, student restaurant Alma will adjust the prices of its meals. The price increases are the result of persistent inflation and a further reduction in the subsidies allocated to Alma by KU Leuven Stuvo (Student Services). The decision was made following consultations between Alma, Stuvo, and student representatives from LOKO.

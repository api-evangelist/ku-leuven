Researchers from KU Leuven and Erasmus MC (the Netherlands) have shown that cells can compensate for the loss of genes on the X chromosome by increasing the expression of the other gene copy. Surprisingly, this compensation occurs with remarkable precision, on a gene-by-gene basis.

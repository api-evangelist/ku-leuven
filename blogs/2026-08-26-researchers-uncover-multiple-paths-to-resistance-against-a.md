Researchers have shown in a new study that resistance to venetoclax, an important precision medicine used to treat leukemia, is conveyed through multiple different routes rather than a single mechanism. The findings provide new insights into why some patients relapse after initially responding well to treatment and how this can be tackled with next-generation medicines.

BIOFIBIX, a spin-off from the Department of Materials Engineering at KU Leuven, developed a unique fibre treatment for processing flax fibres into high-tech composite materials, which makes the process cost-efficient and scalable to larger volumes. In an initial investment round, BIOFIBIX raised €300,000.

A team of researchers from KU Leuven and from the Neurosurgery department at University Hospitals Leuven (UZ Leuven) have, for the first time, succeeded in predicting which visual stimuli a patient will consciously perceive based on the activity of individual brain cells. They could predict this outcome more than one and a half seconds before the patients reported having seen anything.

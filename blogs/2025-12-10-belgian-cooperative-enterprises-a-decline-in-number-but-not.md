Although only 0.14% of Belgian enterprises are cooperatives, they collectively account for 1.1% of Belgium’s GDP and 1.5% of all employment in Belgium. The new ‘Belgian Cooperative Monitor’ by KU Leuven and Cera gives an overview of the Belgian cooperative sector.
